How it works

  1. Request created

    Pick a provider, your preferred times and the reason.

  2. AI preparing

    The agent reviews your details and your free slots.

  3. Calling provider

    The agent introduces itself and asks for your time.

  4. Negotiating

    If your first choice is taken, it offers alternatives.

  5. Appointment confirmed

    You get a notification with the agreed time.

  6. Calendar updated

    The booking lands in your Google Calendar.
